Atelier Guidelines & Restraints

Strict Palette Enforcement:

Blue, purple, green, neon colors, and corporate gradients are permanently forbidden. All visualizations, badges, and controls exclusively render in monochromatic brown and beige gradations.

Typography Pairing:

Headings: Playfair Display & Cormorant Garamond (luxury editorial serif)
Body: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, modern high-contrast sans-serif)

Aesthetic Target:

Independent high-fashion boutique meets bespoke atelier ERP. Minimalist, tactile, warm, feminine without excessive ornament, and pristine.
