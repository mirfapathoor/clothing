# MIFKA — Clothing Boutique & Studio ERP

A bespoke enterprise resource planning (ERP) system created for **MIFKA**, a contemporary clothing boutique & startup studio, engineered with a strict **BROWN + WHITE** visual palette, **Italian Roman** typography, and currency formatted in **Indian Rupee (₹ / INR)**.

---

## Brand & Aesthetic Vision

* **Brand Name**: MIFKA
* **Scale**: Clothing startup boutique & tailoring studio (ready-to-wear & custom tailoring)
* **Pricing Range**: ₹1,950 – ₹4,800 per garment; Fabric sourcing ₹320 – ₹780 per meter
* **Typography**: Italian Roman Didone Serif (`Italiana`, `Bodoni Moda`, `Cinzel`) paired with ultra-clean modern sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)
* **Color Palette**: Strict Deep Espresso Brown (`#3A2118`), Warm Off-White (`#FAF8F6`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Light Beige borders (`#E8DED7`), Dark Brown text (`#2B1A14`), and Soft Brown (`#9A7563`). No blue, purple, green, bright pink, or neon corporate styling.

## Status Badges (Monochromatic Brown & Warm Cream Variations)

* **Payment Pending**: Light beige background (`#F5EFEB`) + brown text (`#6B4636`) + light beige border (`#E8DED7`)
* **Paid**: Light warm cream background (`#FAF5F0`) + dark brown text (`#2B1A14`) + border (`#E8DED7`)
* **In Production**: Soft brown background (`#9A7563`) + white text (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Ready to Ship**: Light beige background (`#EDE5DE`) + dark brown text (`#3A2118`)
* **Delivered**: Very light brown/cream background (`#F7F3EE`) + dark brown text (`#2B1A14`)

## Core Operational Modules

1. **Executive Dashboard**:
   - 3 Operational KPI cards: Active Orders (`18 Garments`), In Stitching (`8 Pieces`), Ready for Pickup / Dispatch (`6 Outfits`).
   - SVG Area Chart: Monthly Orders Completed (`24` to `42` orders fulfilled per month).
   - Donut Chart: Garment category split (Dresses, Co-ord Sets, Tops & Shirts, Skirts & Bottoms).
   - Current Studio Production live tracking table with quick status, measurements drawer, and invoice previews.

2. **Customer Directory**:
   - Clean customer profiles with direct phone numbers, locations (Kochi, Calicut, Bangalore, etc.), order history, and measurements (no arbitrary VIP categorization).

3. **Boutique Invoicing & Ledgers**:
   - Clean startup boutique invoices formatted for print and PDF generation in deep espresso and warm off-white in ₹ INR.
